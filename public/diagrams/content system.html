Spikad 2-Layer Content System (Volume-Based)

Two engines working together: educational content drives massive reach & trust, then CTA content harvests warm attention into bookings. The key is rhythm, not randomness.

Layer 1 Traffic + Trust Layer 2 Conversion + Bookings Goal Predictable Leads
The two engines

Layer 1 — Authority Engine (Educational)

Purpose: reach, saves, shares, trust, audience growth.
Traction magnet
  • 📌
    Topics: Moms/VAT clarity, F-skatt/FA-skatt, bank setup, common mistakes, startup costs, free tools.
  • 📈
    Outcome: high engagement → algorithm push → constant new warm audience entering your page.
  • 🧠
    Positioning: “This guy actually understands Sweden and explains it in our language.”

Layer 2 — Conversion Engine (Direct CTA)

Purpose: DMs, bookings, consultations, package sales.
Harvest attention
  • 🎯
    Format: clear offer + one action (“DM”, “Book”, “Reply with كلمة…”, “Free consult”).
  • ⏱️
    Doesn’t need huge reach: it needs the right audience at the right moment.
  • ✅
    Best when: posted after education waves (warm audience already trusts you).
Core rule: Layer 1 builds pressure + trust. Layer 2 converts it. If Layer 2 is posted without Layer 1 momentum, it still works—but at a lower efficiency.
How it works

1) Educational reel

Attract cold audience with clarity (tax, moms, banks, mistakes). Maximize saves & shares.

➜

2) Warm attention builds

People binge more videos, follow, and start trusting your system.

➜

3) CTA reel

Simple offer + one action. Convert the warmed audience into DMs & bookings.

Weekly cadence (volume-based)

Measure correctly
Layer 1 KPI
Saves + Shares
Signals “value” → increases distribution → fuels your reach engine.
Layer 1 KPI
New Followers
Measures how many cold viewers convert into warm audience.
Layer 2 KPI
DMs / 1,000 views
Best early indicator of offer-message fit and CTA clarity.
Layer 2 KPI
Bookings
Final metric. Optimize by testing CTA script + offer framing.
Scaling rule for volume: keep Layer 1 always-on (daily if possible), keep Layer 2 frequent but sharp (2–3×/week), and never let CTA videos become “noisy”. The audience should feel: “I learned something → now I’m ready to act.”
